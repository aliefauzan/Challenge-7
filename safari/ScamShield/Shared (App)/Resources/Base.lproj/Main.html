ScamShield

ScamShield warns you before you open a known scam website and points out the warning signs of a scam on the pages you read.

Pages are checked on this device. Nothing about your browsing leaves this device.

Turn on ScamShield in Safari

  1. Open the Settings app.
  2. Tap Apps, then Safari, then Extensions.
  3. Tap ScamShield and turn it on.
  4. Under Permissions, tap All Websites and choose Allow.

ScamShield needs to see every website so it can warn you on any of them.
